A counter, not a marketplace.

Zan Top-Up sells one thing: game currency for Free Fire and BGMI, at a rate you can check before you pay.

What we are trying to do

Buying diamonds or UC should take a minute and cost what it says on the label. Too often it takes five, involves an account you did not want, and hides the real rate behind a bundle.

So the whole site is one page of packs with the price per 100 units printed on every card, a payment link, and a chat thread where a person confirms your order. That is the entire product.

We would rather be the place a player comes back to for the tenth time than the place that squeezed the most out of the first.

How the counter is run

Five commitments that shape every order.

  • Rates are shown, not implied. Every pack carries its price per 100 units so you can compare sizes honestly.
  • No account, no wallet. You never create a login here and we never hold a balance on your behalf.
  • Payments stay with the provider. UPI runs inside your own payment app; cards go through a hosted page. We do not take card numbers.
  • One thread per order. Your player ID, amount and payment proof sit in the same chat, so nothing has to be explained twice.
  • We say when we cannot help. A top-up sent to a mistyped ID cannot be recalled, and we will tell you that plainly rather than stall.

Where we operate

Orders are priced and settled in Indian rupees, and support runs on Indian hours. Free Fire diamonds and BGMI UC are the two products on the counter today.

If you want a game added, say so on the contact page. Requests decide what gets stocked next.

    Add the next game based on what people request on the contact page, starting with the most repeated ask.
